Junior year Spring Term

This part consists some of the projects I have completed during the Spring Term.

Talking Clean

A website that consists for a game named Talking Clean that me and my group made for the Infy App Challenge. You play as the player Doobie and learn about the environment. You are lead by the guide, Sir-Quacks-A-Lot, who gives you instructions and says if you are right or wrong.

This was a project me and my team worked for almost 5-6 months.

My role in the team was project manager. Over the project period, I ensured my group members were completing their work and we all stayed on top of our tasks. I also worked with the UX and UI leads by providing them with ideas for our game such as what trash there could be, how we could make the user click on trash cans to see if they knew which trash belongs where. I also helped with prototyping. Something that I learned was during our research on how a lot of people enjoy point based games.

The most difficult part of the project for me personally was ensuring everyone was doing their work. There were times where specific people would not do their work or the part I asked them to finish by time which would then set us back. This had led to a lot of challenges and then I had to distribute the task to other group members.I would probably stick with one concept as we changed our concept halfway through and it caused us to cram everything. It also led to a lot of stress among the group members.

Skills: HTML & CSS, Figma, Collaboration. Canva

Space Driver

A 2D shooter game where in level 1 the rocketship must move around avoiding the attacks from the enemies whilst shooting the enemies at the same time. Once they destroy all the enemies they can proceed to the next level. In Level 2 along with the enemies from the previous level, they must defeat aliens too. Once they destroy all the enemies on this floor, they win the game. The player uses the WASD keys to move around and left clicking on the mouse to shoot bullets. They also hover the mouse over the scene to move the rocketship around so that they can attack the aliens.

Skills: HTML & CSS
